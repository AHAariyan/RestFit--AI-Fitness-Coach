Restfit · Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · App: Restfit: AI Fitness Coach (Android, package com.matrabhed.steady)

In short: Restfit plans home workouts around how you slept and how you feel. Your plan, check-ins, sleep times, meals and workouts are stored only on your phone. There is no account. The camera rep counter works on your phone: frames are processed and dropped at once, never saved and never uploaded. We don't sell data and there is no advertising. The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which never contain your words, times or health details. You can turn them off at any time in the app.

1. Who we are

Restfit is an Android app published by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact details are at the end of this policy.

2. What you enter, and where it stays

Restfit stores the following only on your device, to build and adapt your workout plan:

DataWhat Restfit uses it for
Your answers when you start: goal, fitness level (and push-ups from the optional level test), training days and session length, rest days, usual bed and wake times, and any injuries or limitsBuilding your weekly plan and keeping limited areas eased or excluded
Morning check-ins: bedtime, wake time, how you feel, sorenessDeciding whether today is a hard, easy or rest day
Workouts: moves, reps, sets, how a session felt, "something hurts" reportsYour history, progress, and keeping a sore area out of your plan
Meal checks (a simple plate check and a fullness scale)The mindful-eating view. No calories and no food photos
Steps during a guided walk, from your phone's step sensorShowing steps and pace for that walk
Messages you type to the coachAnswering them on your phone with Restfit's coaching rules. They are never sent anywhere
Settings and remindersYour preferences, the morning check-in and the night wind-down reminder

This data is never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see it. It is excluded from Android cloud backup. If you move to a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer, it can be copied directly to that phone.

3. Camera

The camera rep counter is optional. When you use it, a pose model running on your phone looks at your movement to count reps. Frames are processed and dropped at once: nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded, and no human or server ever sees them. Restfit never asks for photos of you or your food. If you don't allow the camera, you can count reps by tapping instead.

4. Physical activity permission

During a guided walk, Restfit can use Android's "Physical activity" permission to read your phone's step sensor, so it can show steps and pace for that walk. The counts stay on your phone. You can deny or remove this permission at any time; the walk still works without step counts.

5. The coach

"Ask the coach" runs entirely on your phone, without internet. Your messages are matched to Restfit's coaching rules; they are not sent to an AI service or to us, and nothing in your plan changes until you confirm. The "AI" in Restfit refers to the on-device pose model and the plan that adapts to you.

6. Usage statistics and crash reports

To learn which features help, where people get stuck and why the app crashes, Restfit uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, both provided by Google. This is on by default. You can turn it off at any time in the app: Profile → Privacy → "Share anonymous usage".

These reports contain codes for what happened in the app, never your words, times or health values. Examples: a screen was opened, a check-in was finished, a workout started or ended, the plan was adjusted (the kind of adjustment), the coach was asked a question (only its topic category, such as "sleep" or "pain", never what you wrote), the Pro screen was opened, a purchase succeeded or was cancelled, and whether you have Pro.

Firebase also collects standard technical information: device model, Android version, app version, country, and a random app-instance identifier. Crash reports contain the technical state of the app when it crashed. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy and the Firebase privacy information. Restfit does not use the advertising ID.

7. Purchases

Restfit Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns whether you have an active subscription, so it can unlock Pro. You can cancel at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

8. What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No location tracking.
  • No body photos, food photos or recordings of you.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Restfit has no account, so there is no profile on our side to close. You can delete everything yourself, at any time:

  • Your plan, check-ins, workouts, meals and settings live only on your phone. Delete them by uninstalling Restfit, or in Android Settings → Apps → Restfit → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports: in Restfit, open Profile → Privacy and turn off Share anonymous usage. This stops collection and resets the random app-instance identifier, so statistics already sent can no longer be linked to your phone. Google Analytics keeps usage statistics for up to 14 months, then deletes them automatically.
  • Ask us: email abdulhady.aariyan@gmail.com with the subject "Restfit data deletion request". We will reply within 30 days and delete any data we hold that can be linked to you.

10. Health and safety

Restfit is for general fitness and wellbeing. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. If pain is sharp or lasting, see a doctor.

11. Children

Restfit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how Restfit handles data, we will update this page and its date before the change ships. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
